# Organizational Financials

This folder contains Harmony for Hope, Inc.’s organizational financial materials prepared for grant, partnership, and due-diligence review.

Harmony for Hope is a small West Virginia nonprofit corporation and federally recognized 501(c)(3) public charity. The organization files the IRS Form 990-N (e-Postcard) annually and prepares financial materials appropriate to its size, operations, and grant-application needs.

## Contents

- [FY2025 Financial Statement](Financial_Statement_FY2025.md)  
  Unaudited, cash-basis financial statements for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, including a Statement of Financial Position, Statement of Activities, and explanatory notes.

- [FY2025 Financial Statement PDF](Financial_Statement_FY2025.pdf)  
  PDF version of the FY2025 financial statement prepared for grant-application and external-review purposes.

## Financial-Statement Status

The FY2025 financial statement is:

- Prepared on the cash basis of accounting.
- Unaudited.
- Prepared by Harmony for Hope’s authorized representative for grant-application purposes.
- Intended to present the organization’s reported fiscal-year cash activity and year-end financial position accurately.

The statement does not represent an independent audit, reviewed financial statement, forecast, or guarantee of future revenue.

## Separation of Financial Materials

This folder contains **organization-level financial information**. It should not be confused with the proposed New River Gorge Safety & Heritage Mesh Pilot budget.

The project’s first-year pilot budget is maintained separately at:

```text
../../03_project/pilot_budget/Master_First_Year_Pilot_Budget.md
```

Harmony for Hope distinguishes among:

- Organizational financial activity and year-end financial position.
- Project-specific proposed budgets.
- In-kind contributions and volunteer labor.
- Pending, prospective, or unawarded grant applications.
- Developing MountainShares or other future program models.

Only documented cash revenue and expenses are reflected in the FY2025 financial statement unless expressly stated otherwise.

## Grant and Due-Diligence Use

These materials may be used by prospective funders, public agencies, collaborators, and other authorized reviewers to evaluate Harmony for Hope’s organizational financial position and grant readiness.

For information about a specific project budget, grant request, award, restricted contribution, or supporting financial record, contact Harmony for Hope through its official organizational contact information.
