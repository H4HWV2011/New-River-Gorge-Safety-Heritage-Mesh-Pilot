# Organization and Governance

This folder contains Harmony for Hope, Inc.'s core organizational, nonprofit-status, governance, related-party, and program-history materials prepared for grant, partnership, funder, university, and organizational-review purposes.

Harmony for Hope is a West Virginia nonprofit corporation and federally recognized **501(c)(3) public charity**.

The materials in this section are intended to help reviewers understand:

* the organization's nonprofit status;
* current Board structure;
* governance safeguards;
* related-party service arrangements;
* organizational history;
* community-program experience; and
* the distinction between public portfolio records and Harmony for Hope's controlling official records.

## Governance Principles

Harmony for Hope's governance approach is guided by the following principles:

* **Board oversight:** Material organizational decisions remain subject to appropriate Board authority.
* **Clear roles:** Officer, Board, contractor, nonprofit, and technical-platform responsibilities should be distinguishable.
* **Conflict disclosure:** Actual, potential, and apparent conflicts should be disclosed promptly.
* **Recusal:** Interested persons should not control deliberation or voting on matters in which they have a material conflict.
* **Disinterested review:** Related-party matters should be reviewed by appropriately disinterested decision-makers.
* **Documentation:** Material governance, contracting, conflict, and related-party actions should be recorded.
* **Reasonable terms:** Related-party arrangements should serve the organization's charitable interests and be reasonable for the services provided.
* **Public benefit:** Governance decisions should protect Harmony for Hope's charitable purpose and 501(c)(3) obligations.
* **Accurate status:** Draft policies, proposed agreements, executed documents, Board actions, and working portfolio copies should not be represented as equivalent.
* **Periodic review:** Governance procedures should develop as the organization's programs, funding, partnerships, and operational responsibilities grow.

## Organizational and Technical Roles

The New River Gorge Safety & Heritage Mesh Pilot involves related but distinct organizational roles.

### Harmony for Hope, Inc.

Harmony for Hope is the nonprofit program sponsor.

Its responsibilities may include:

* charitable program oversight;
* fundraising and grant administration;
* community relationships;
* public-benefit stewardship;
* program evaluation;
* organizational governance;
* partner coordination; and
* accountability for funds awarded directly to the nonprofit.

### Kidd's Technical Services

Kidd's Technical Services is the developer/operator associated with ALLIS and related technical services.

Any paid technical work performed for Harmony for Hope is subject to the applicable:

* services agreement;
* statement of work;
* conflict-of-interest process;
* Board review;
* funding restrictions;
* procurement expectations; and
* documentation requirements.

### Board of Directors

Harmony for Hope's Board retains nonprofit governance authority according to the organization's governing documents and applicable law.

Technical ownership, software development, contractor status, founder status, or operational involvement does not substitute for Board governance where Board action is required.
