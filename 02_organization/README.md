# Governance

This folder contains Harmony for Hope, Inc.’s core governance materials prepared for grant, partnership, and organizational-review purposes.

Harmony for Hope is a West Virginia nonprofit corporation and federally recognized 501(c)(3) public charity. These records are provided to document the organization’s current Board structure and its approach to transparent, accountable decision-making.

The Conflict-of-Interest and Recusal Policy is currently identified as:

> **Draft — For Board Adoption and Counsel Review**

It is included to document Harmony for Hope’s governance-readiness work and intended safeguards for related-party decisions. The policy will not be represented as a formally adopted Board instrument until Board adoption is completed and recorded.

## Governance Principles

Harmony for Hope’s governance approach is guided by the following principles:

- Clear Board oversight and defined officer roles.
- Timely disclosure of actual, potential, or apparent conflicts of interest.
- Recusal by interested persons from deliberation and voting on conflicted matters.
- Review and approval of related-party matters by disinterested Directors.
- Documentation of material governance actions and related-party decisions.
- Protection of the organization’s charitable purpose, public benefit, and 501(c)(3) obligations.
- Periodic review of governance procedures as the organization’s programs and partnerships develop.
