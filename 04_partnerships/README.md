# Partnerships and Readiness

Harmony for Hope, Inc. is building the New River Gorge Safety & Heritage Mesh Pilot through place-based collaboration.

This directory preserves the **public documentary record of agreements and written support** associated with:

* the initial Mount Hope implementation path;
* development of Thurmond as a future federal-phase opportunity;
* technical, research, communications, and regional-development relationships; and
* broader project readiness.

The directory also summarizes referrals and developing collaboration pathways that are relevant to project development but are **not represented as formal agreements unless the repository contains documentation establishing that status**.

---

## Purpose of This Directory

The materials in this directory help partners, funders, universities, agencies, and other reviewers understand the project's partnership and implementation readiness.

Different forms of relationship evidence should be interpreted differently.

| Relationship evidence   | What it establishes                                                                     |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Signed agreement        | A formal relationship within the actual terms of the agreement                          |
| Letter of support       | Written support for the project, concept, organization, or relevant goals               |
| Referral                | A connection or recommendation to pursue a resource, opportunity, or assistance pathway |
| Developing relationship | Communication, collaboration, or goodwill that may support future work                  |
| Informal discussion     | Evidence of communication only, unless later documented more formally                   |

No relationship should be represented as creating an obligation beyond the terms of the evidence that actually exists.

In particular:

```text
letter of support
        ≠
funding

letter of support
        ≠
site authorization

relationship
        ≠
formal institutional commitment

referral
        ≠
award

planning collaboration
        ≠
implementation authority
```

## Support Boundary

Letters of support are evidence of documented support.

They should not be interpreted as automatically establishing:

* grant funding;
* procurement authority;
* site access;
* property rights;
* installation permission;
* permitting;
* technical approval;
* federal approval;
* operating responsibility;
* or commitment to fund future phases.

A support letter should always be interpreted within the authority and wording of the underlying document.

# Thurmond Partnership Path

**Thurmond is a future federal-phase implementation opportunity.**

Harmony for Hope has documented written support relevant to continued project development from:

* the Town of Thurmond; and
* New River Gorge National Park and Preserve.

That support provides meaningful evidence that the concept has moved beyond an entirely internal idea.

It does **not** constitute:

```text
support
    =
federal authorization
```

or:

```text
support
    =
approved construction scope
```

A future Thurmond implementation remains contingent on:

* an appropriate federal funding pathway;
* a defined site-specific project scope;
* National Park Service review and compliance;
* site-specific approval;
* technical review;
* preservation and accessibility requirements as applicable;
* privacy and information-governance review;
* required agreements;
* and final authorization before implementation.

Depending on the eventual scope, federal processes may include cultural-resource, permitting, technical, environmental, accessibility, cybersecurity, records, or other review requirements.

Those requirements should be identified according to the actual future project and applicable federal process rather than assumed in advance.

# Relationship Status Discipline

Partnership records should use the most precise supported description available.

A useful progression is:

```text
conversation
    ↓
relationship development
    ↓
referral / documented interest
    ↓
letter of support
    ↓
agreement, if executed
    ↓
defined project role
    ↓
funded / authorized role, if applicable
    ↓
implementation
```

Not every relationship will move through every stage.

The repository should not imply that it has.

# Partnership Principles

Harmony for Hope approaches partnerships according to the following principles:

* **Accuracy:** Describe every relationship according to what is actually documented.
* **Authority:** Do not assign a partner authority, responsibility, or commitment beyond the underlying evidence.
* **Local stewardship:** Keep community partners involved in decisions affecting local sites, stories, and public use.
* **Respect for approvals:** Deployment remains subject to applicable site permissions, technical review, agreements, funding, and approvals.
* **Public benefit:** Develop infrastructure intended to serve residents, visitors, institutions, and local community life.
* **Federal readiness:** Treat federal-site opportunities as separate implementation pathways requiring appropriate funding, review, compliance, and authorization.
* **Responsible expansion:** Use Mount Hope to generate evidence before attempting broader replication.
* **Evidence before claims:** Support, referrals, planning, and relationships demonstrate readiness only to the extent actually supported by the record.
* **Provenance:** Preserve signed agreements and support letters as historical evidence rather than rewriting them to match later project development.
* **Status discipline:** Distinguish relationship development, written support, agreements, funding, approved scope, implementation, and outcomes.

# Partnership Stewardship Principle

The purpose of this section is not to make the project appear to have more partners than it does.

It is to make the partnership record **traceable and credible**.

The governing discipline is:

```text
document the relationship
        ↓
identify what the evidence actually establishes
        ↓
do not enlarge the partner's commitment
        ↓
separate support from authority
        ↓
separate authority from implementation
        ↓
preserve the record as the project evolves
```

That distinction allows Harmony for Hope to demonstrate substantial community, regional, university, media, and public-sector support without overstating what any individual partner has agreed to fund, approve, or implement.
