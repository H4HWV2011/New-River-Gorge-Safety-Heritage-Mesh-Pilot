# Master first-year pilot budget

## New River Gorge Safety & Heritage Mesh Pilot

**Program Sponsor:** Harmony for Hope, Inc.
**First Public Anchor:** Fayette County Community Arts Center, Mount Hope, West Virginia
**Budget Period:** First 12 months following award or project start
**Budget Status:** Planning estimate for grant development. Written agreements, site review, procurement, and funder requirements may change the final costs below.

---

## Investment summary

Harmony for Hope, Inc. seeks support for the first year of the New River Gorge Safety & Heritage Mesh Pilot: a community-governed public connectivity and local-information proof of concept anchored at the Fayette County Community Arts Center in Mount Hope.

The budget funds a practical public deployment: equipment and installation, a public-facing local-information experience, connectivity service, legal and governance support, professional project administration, and a six-month paid application and community testing cohort.

| Investment area | Amount |
|---|---:|
| First-year public pilot | $30,684 |
| Six-month application and community testing cohort | $30,000 |
| **Total first-year investment** | **$60,684** |

## Application and community testing cohort

A six-month paid cohort of five specialists will verify that the pilot's public-facing systems work correctly, and will evaluate the deployment from the standpoint of real users, local institutions, and community stewards.

| Expense item | Amount | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| QA/Test Lead stipend (1 person, 6 months) | $6,000 | Coordinates the test plan, tracks defects, and reports findings to the project team |
| Application and browser testers (4 people, 6 months, part-time) | $16,000 | Test the MountainShares/ALLIS public-facing application across desktop and mobile browsers, including Chrome, Safari, and Firefox |
| Test devices (laptops, tablets, phones) | $3,000 | Give testers cross-device and cross-browser coverage that matches real visitor conditions |
| Field testing travel and mileage | $2,000 | Fund on-site verification visits to the Mount Hope/FCCAC anchor after installers complete the hardware build |
| Test documentation and bug-tracking tools/licenses | $1,000 | Provide structured issue tracking and reporting infrastructure for the testing period |
| Testing contingency allowance | $2,000 | Cover unanticipated testing, device, or coordination needs |
| **Application and community testing total** | **$30,000** | |

### Testing and evaluation activities

The testing cohort will document:

- Public access, usability, and accessibility of the MountainShares/ALLIS application, including cross-browser and cross-device functionality.
- On-site verification that the Mount Hope/FCCAC public Wi-Fi mesh and locally hosted content load correctly on real visitor devices.
- The quality, relevance, and gaps in local heritage and safety information that the application presents.
- The privacy, stewardship, and public-use expectations that real users bring to the pilot.
- Reliability and resilience observations during the pilot period.
- Issues, recommendations, and a final testing and evaluation memo that guides responsible next steps.

## Responsible stewardship

Harmony for Hope will use all funds according to applicable grant requirements, approved project scopes, written agreements, procurement expectations, conflict-of-interest procedures, and reporting obligations.

This work is a phased proof of concept. The budget funds a practical first-year pilot and testing process; it does not claim that a corridor-wide network is already deployed.
