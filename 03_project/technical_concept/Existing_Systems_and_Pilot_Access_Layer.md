# Existing Systems and Pilot Access Layer

## Purpose

The New River Gorge Safety & Heritage Mesh Pilot builds on existing Harmony for Hope digital-development work while proposing a separate, modest physical public-access deployment.

This document distinguishes existing systems, publicly viewable pre-launch systems, and proposed funded infrastructure so that the project is described accurately.

## Existing Development Foundation

Harmony for Hope has developed and maintains Ms. Allis, a community-information and location-intelligence system that supports local knowledge development, place-based information organization, research, and preparation for future community-serving digital infrastructure.

Ms. Allis is developed through Harmony for Hope’s local technical environment and has a public online presence at [egeria.mountainshares.us](https://egeria.mountainshares.us/).

The public availability of an online interface does not mean that a public Wi‑Fi mesh, corridor-wide network, marketplace, payment system, or community-node deployment is currently operating.

## The Commons: Publicly Viewable, Phase 0 Pre-Launch

[The Commons](https://egeria.mountainshares.us/commons) is a publicly viewable MountainShares marketplace interface and local-information environment.

The Commons displays the planned structure for local listings, goods, services, events, community notices, requests, and other forms of neighbor-to-neighbor participation. It is currently in **Phase 0 pre-launch** status.

The following functions are not currently represented as live public operations:

- Active marketplace listings.
- Verified Champion participation.
- Dollar or MountainShares transactions.
- Public MountainShares payment functions.
- Open public accounts for marketplace participation.
- County-wide marketplace operations.

The Commons may become a future locally governed participation and information resource, but its operational activation is separate from the first public Wi‑Fi pilot.

## Proposed Public-Access Layer

The first funded mesh-pilot phase is intended to establish free public Wi‑Fi and a locally stewarded information-access point at the Fayette County Community Arts Center in Mount Hope.

The proposed access layer would enable residents and visitors to reach useful online resources, including public-service information, arts and community programming, local events, heritage interpretation, visitor resources, and other publicly available materials.

The first phase does not require an active marketplace, MountainShares payment function, DAO operation, or corridor-wide mesh network.

## Stewardship and Operating Roles

Harmony for Hope, Inc. is the current charitable sponsor and steward of the proposed pilot. The organization is responsible for project planning, grant administration, site and partner coordination, public-benefit oversight, and responsible deployment planning.

MountainShares Commons is a developing long-term framework for community participation, value recognition, and local stewardship. It is not represented as an available grant match, independently funded operating entity, public Wi‑Fi operator, or active financial system for the first pilot.

## Expansion Conditions

Any future connection between the public-access layer, The Commons, MountainShares participation tools, the Mount Hope Historic Walking Trail, Thurmond, or other heritage-sensitive locations will depend on appropriate permissions, partner coordination, funding, technical planning, privacy and stewardship safeguards, operating capacity, and demonstrated public benefit.

## Public-Release Boundary

This document intentionally describes the pilot at a public-concept level. Detailed network design, device configuration, credentials, security controls, system internals, and sensitive operational information are not included in the public grant portfolio.
