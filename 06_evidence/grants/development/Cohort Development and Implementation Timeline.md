# Mount Hope Founding Cohort Development and Implementation Timeline

## Document status

- **Project:** West Virginia Community Champion Leadership Cohort Series — Mount Hope Founding Cohort
- **Organization:** Harmony for Hope, Inc.
- **Status:** Planning and development
- **Target implementation window:** Fall 2027 through Summer 2028
- **Scheduling condition:** No public delivery date, registration, or participant commitment will be announced until core funding, venue/access arrangements, delivery safeguards, and the cohort-ready MountainShares Commons workflow are confirmed.
- **Last updated:** September 7, 2026

## Purpose

This timeline sets out a measured path from the current planning stage to one small, safe, documented Mount Hope Founding Cohort. It does not represent a commitment to deploy the full New River Gorge Safety & Heritage Mesh Pilot, launch a statewide platform, or begin doctoral research.

The Founding Cohort is intended to test a practical Community Champion leadership and stewardship pathway in a real place-based setting. It will be delivered only at a scale that Harmony for Hope can responsibly supervise, fund, document, and improve.

## Core implementation principles

1. **No personal out-of-pocket launch.** The cohort will not be scheduled or opened for registration until direct delivery costs are covered through confirmed project funds, written sponsor commitments, confirmed in-kind support, or a documented combination of those sources.
2. **Truthful app status.** MountainShares Commons is in active development. Cohort materials will distinguish clearly among available, prototype, planned, and unavailable functions.
3. **No implied authority.** Cohort participation does not make a person an emergency responder, government official, inspector, property representative, technical network operator, or representative of a partner agency.
4. **Safety, privacy, and accessibility first.** Participants will not be asked to enter unsafe, restricted, private, or inaccessible locations, collect unnecessary personal data, or use the app as an emergency system.
5. **Mount Hope first.** The Founding Cohort is a bounded first implementation. Future communities and statewide expansion are separate decisions after review of funding, local fit, governance, and lessons learned.
6. **Program evaluation is not doctoral research.** Routine implementation records and program feedback may be used to improve delivery. Any future human-subjects research use will follow applicable institutional guidance, consent, and ethics-review requirements.

## Phase 1: Definition and documentation

**Timeframe:** September–December 2026

### Objectives

- Define the smallest credible Mount Hope Founding Cohort.
- Clarify what participants will learn and what Community Champion participation means.
- Document the actual current state of MountainShares Commons.
- Establish the financial boundary that direct costs will not come from an individual organizer’s personal funds.

### Deliverables

- Mount Hope Founding Cohort one-page overview.
- Community Champion role description, including authority, privacy, safety, and escalation boundaries.
- `Founding Cohort App Status and Learning Scope` document identifying available, prototype, planned, and unavailable app functions.
- Draft four-hour curriculum outline and learning outcomes.
- Draft MountainShares Commons app-use guide for Community Champions.
- First minimum viable cohort budget, separating core costs from optional enhancements.
- Initial funding map identifying potential grants, sponsors, in-kind support, and fiscal/financial handling needs.
- Initial list of Mount Hope venue, route, heritage, accessibility, and community contacts for future validation.

### Exit criteria

Proceed to Phase 2 only when Harmony for Hope can accurately explain:

- Who the cohort serves.
- What participants will do during the cohort.
- What MountainShares Commons can and cannot do at the cohort stage.
- What the cohort costs at minimum.
- What participation does not authorize a Community Champion to do.

## Phase 2: Design, prototyping, and local validation preparation

**Timeframe:** January–June 2027

### Objectives

- Turn the concept into a runnable operating packet.
- Test the intended participant workflow before any public recruitment.
- Prepare materials for factual and practical review by appropriate local stakeholders.

### Deliverables

- Detailed run of show for a four-hour cohort.
- Facilitator guide, including roles, timing, materials, accessibility practices, weather backup, incident response, and closeout steps.
- Participant orientation materials, acknowledgment of boundaries, and plain-language privacy guidance.
- Guided MountainShares Commons prototype or limited workflow for cohort use.
- Structured practice observation/reporting template with required and prohibited information clearly identified.
- Readiness rubric distinguishing attendance, completed exercises, demonstrated judgment, and any future continued participation.
- Draft participant interest/registration form that collects only necessary information.
- Draft program feedback and implementation-closeout tools.
- Initial route plan and venue requirements checklist.
- Updated line-item budget using real quotes or documented cost assumptions.

### Local validation questions

- Is the Mount Hope route accurate, lawful, and appropriate for the planned activity?
- Are heritage descriptions, place names, and local context factually and respectfully presented?
- Are accessibility constraints and weather risks identified honestly?
- Are the app workflow, reporting categories, privacy rules, and escalation boundaries understandable to a first-time participant?
- Do any venue, route, municipal, property, insurance, or partner approvals need to be obtained before public delivery?

### Exit criteria

Proceed to Phase 3 when the program packet is sufficiently complete for funders and local reviewers to understand the scope, costs, safeguards, and intended outputs.

## Phase 3: Funding, commitments, and delivery-window selection

**Timeframe:** July–December 2027

### Objectives

- Secure the resources required to deliver the cohort without personal cash subsidy.
- Convert exploratory relationships into accurate, written commitments where applicable.
- Choose a target delivery date only after readiness conditions are met.

### Funding actions

- Submit focused requests for direct delivery costs: venue, materials, accessibility/safety needs, required fees, modest outreach, and appropriate facilitation/administration costs.
- Seek confirmed in-kind contributions only where they are documented and usable on the proposed date.
- Maintain a distinction among confirmed cash, confirmed in-kind support, pending requests, and unconfirmed possibilities.
- Preserve a modest contingency in the budget for foreseeable delivery changes.

### Commitment actions

- Confirm venue, date window, rental terms, setup needs, accessibility, cancellation terms, and payment requirements.
- Confirm route conditions, local factual review, and any permissions or coordination needed.
- Confirm facilitator/support roles and identify a backup plan for key absences.
- Confirm any required insurance, permits, participant waivers, or venue policies.
- Confirm the cohort-day MountainShares Commons workflow and who is responsible for support, moderation, review, and any follow-up.

### Delivery readiness gate

A public cohort date may be selected only when all of the following are true:

- Core direct costs are covered through confirmed project funding and/or confirmed in-kind support.
- A venue and delivery date are confirmed in writing.
- The participant workflow is tested and accurately documented.
- Required safety, accessibility, privacy, and escalation procedures are complete.
- Local content and route information have received appropriate factual review.
- The program has sufficient facilitators/support to serve the planned cohort size.

If these conditions are not met by the end of 2027, the target shifts into Spring or Summer 2028 rather than requiring personal financial risk or rushed delivery.

## Phase 4: Final preparation and recruitment

**Timeframe:** January–June 2028, or the 8–12 weeks preceding the confirmed event date

### Objectives

- Open recruitment only for a cohort that can be delivered safely and well.
- Prepare every participant, facilitator, material, and contingency before the event.

### Deliverables

- Final public cohort description using only confirmed claims.
- Registration/selection process and participant communications.
- Final agenda, participant packet, facilitator packet, app-use guide, route plan, maps, and supplies list.
- Confirmed attendance cap based on supervision, route, venue, accessibility, and app-support capacity.
- Participant reminders covering clothing, weather, timing, transportation, accessibility needs, emergency procedures, and authority/privacy boundaries.
- Printed or offline backup materials for technology/connectivity failure.
- Day-of incident, weather, cancellation, and communication plan.

### Recruitment standard

Recruitment should target a small, manageable Founding Cohort. Quality of support, supervision, and follow-through matters more than enrollment volume. No participant should be recruited on the assumption that the app is a finished public platform or that Community Champion training confers official authority.

## Phase 5: Cohort delivery

**Timeframe:** Fall 2027 through Summer 2028, on the confirmed date

### Cohort-day sequence

1. Welcome, purpose, Community Champion role, and boundaries.
2. Mount Hope place-based orientation and respectful local engagement.
3. Guided MountainShares Commons orientation using the available cohort workflow or clearly labeled prototype.
4. Privacy, safety, authority, verification, and escalation practice.
5. Supervised field observation or route-based practice activity.
6. Structured reporting exercise and facilitated review.
7. Participant feedback, closeout, and explanation of next steps.

### Required delivery conditions

- The cohort remains free to participants unless a future written policy says otherwise.
- No participant is expected to use personal funds for required program materials.
- No personal organizer is expected to fund venue or required materials from personal resources.
- Emergency situations are directed to 911 and appropriate official channels, not the application.
- Public posting, identifiable photography, and data collection occur only under the documented permissions and privacy rules.

## Phase 6: Closeout, learning, and next decision

**Timeframe:** Within 60 days after delivery

### Document

- Final attendance and completion counts.
- Actual cash and in-kind expenditures compared with budget.
- Materials used and replenishment needs.
- App/workflow issues, usability feedback, and prioritized revisions.
- Facilitator observations concerning timing, safety, accessibility, clarity, and participant support.
- Routine participant program feedback, handled in accordance with the published privacy practices.
- Lessons about venue, route, recruitment, partnerships, and support capacity.
- A short implementation learning memo identifying what worked, what did not, and what should change before another cohort.

### Decide

After closeout, Harmony for Hope will decide whether to:

- Refine and repeat the Mount Hope cohort.
- Adapt the model for a different West Virginia community.
- Pause pending additional funding, partnership, application, or capacity development.
- Explore whether the mature program creates an appropriate future Leadership Studies Ed.D. problem-of-practice research setting.

## Change-control rule

This is a planning document, not a promise of a fixed event date. Any major change to cohort scope, app capabilities, public claims, partner role, participant data practice, cost, or delivery window must be recorded here and reflected in the budget, curriculum, participant communications, and relevant governance/privacy documents.
