# Evidence

This folder contains documentary evidence supporting the Harmony for Hope Community Connectivity & Local Knowledge Portfolio.

Evidence materials demonstrate prior community work, public-benefit programming, heritage interpretation, local partnerships, place-based knowledge, and the organizational experience that informs the proposed New River Gorge Safety & Heritage Mesh Pilot.

## Evidence Categories

### Community Programs

Community-program materials document public-facing work connected to arts, heritage, youth, volunteer service, local information, and community development.

- [Mount Hope Historic Walking Trail](community_programs/Mount_Hope_Historic_Walking_Trail/README.md)  
  Historic walking-tour brochure and attribution record documenting collaborative prior heritage-interpretation and visitor-information work in Mount Hope.

### Site Photos

This section will contain photographs documenting relevant public-access locations, including the Fayette County Community Arts Center, Mount Hope streetscape, historic-walking-trail locations, and other sites relevant to the proposed pilot.

### Maps

This section will contain public-facing maps, site-context materials, and geographic references that help reviewers understand the relationship among Mount Hope, the Fayette County Community Arts Center, the Mount Hope Historic Walking Trail, Thurmond, and the New River Gorge region.

### Media and Testimonials

This section will contain selected public media, partner statements, community feedback, testimonials, and other materials that document community relevance and public benefit.

## Evidence Use

These materials are included to demonstrate existing experience and community context. They are not intended to represent that the proposed mesh network, public Wi‑Fi system, corridor-wide infrastructure, or future participation tools are already installed or operational.

Where possible, each item identifies its source, date, authorship, permission status, and relationship to the portfolio.

## Public-Release Standard

Harmony for Hope includes evidence only after considering accuracy, attribution, privacy, permissions, and public-release suitability. Materials involving third-party copyright, personal information, or sensitive site details may be summarized, redacted, or retained outside the public portfolio.
