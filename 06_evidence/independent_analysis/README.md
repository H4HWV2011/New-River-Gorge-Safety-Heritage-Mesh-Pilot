# Independent Analysis

This directory contains **independently prepared analytical reports and decision-support materials** that are relevant to the broader Harmony for Hope Community Connectivity & Local Knowledge Portfolio, its communities, or related public-interest questions.

The purpose of this directory is to preserve useful original analysis **without falsely representing that every report was produced by Harmony for Hope, funded through an H4H project, adopted by H4H, or included within an approved project scope**.

`independent_analysis/` can eventually contain more than one report without falsely making those reports part of the funded H4H project.

---

## Purpose

Some analyses may be highly relevant to:

- Mount Hope;
- Thurmond;
- New River Gorge communities;
- heritage preservation;
- tourism and economic development;
- community infrastructure;
- public-sector decision making;
- local knowledge;
- governance;
- community stewardship;
- leadership;
- financing;
- or future project development.

Relevance to the portfolio does not make an independent analysis an H4H project deliverable.

This directory provides a place to preserve those materials while maintaining clear provenance and status boundaries.

---

## What Belongs Here

Materials may be included when they are:

- independently prepared by Kidd's Technical Services or another clearly identified author or entity;
- substantively relevant to a community, issue, or development pathway represented in the portfolio;
- useful as research, analysis, decision support, or future project-development evidence;
- appropriate for public release; and
- clearly distinguishable from H4H grant applications, funded deliverables, adopted policies, project budgets, or implementation records.

Examples may include:

- economic analyses;
- preservation-investment studies;
- feasibility analyses;
- place-based assessments;
- infrastructure analyses;
- decision-support reports;
- leadership or governance analyses;
- spatial or GIS studies;
- tourism and market analyses;
- and other independently produced reports relevant to the portfolio.

---

## What Inclusion Does Not Mean

The presence of a report in this directory does **not** establish that:

- Harmony for Hope commissioned the report;
- Harmony for Hope funded the report;
- the report is part of the current New River Gorge Safety & Heritage Mesh Pilot budget;
- the report is an H4H grant deliverable;
- H4H adopted every conclusion or recommendation;
- a public agency adopted the analysis;
- a partner endorsed its findings;
- funding was awarded;
- implementation was authorized;
- or the recommendations described in the report were carried out.

Inclusion means only that the analysis is sufficiently relevant to the portfolio to preserve as part of its broader evidence and learning record.
